At Dr. Ali Children's Clinic, we are dedicated to providing exceptional pediatric care and specialized nutritional guidance to children in Aurangabad and surrounding areas. Our mission is to ensure every child receives comprehensive healthcare in a warm, caring environment.

Led by Dr. Ghazanfar Ali Syed, an experienced Pediatrician & Neonatologist with international healthcare expertise, and Dr. Naziya Syed, a qualified Clinical Nutritionist, we offer integrated healthcare solutions that address both medical and nutritional needs of your child.
